How it works

You bring a Claude API key from Anthropic (pay as you go, usually pennies per job). Everything runs on your PC and your keys stay encrypted there.

Give a brief, press RunULTRON plans the job and each bot builds on the last. Or just tell ULTRON in plain English.
Rooms hand work to each otherResearch feeds Sales, a podcast plan goes to the Video Studio, findings become Store designs.
Approve from your phoneLink Telegram and tap Approve, Redo or Send from anywhere. Message ULTRON on the go.
Bots that actually do thingsSend emails (you approve each one), post summaries to Slack or Discord, save results to your Drive folder.
Talk to ULTRONHit the mic and speak; ULTRON can read his replies out loud.
You stay in controlPer-room daily budgets, a cost readout on every bot, and you choose which Claude model each one uses.
